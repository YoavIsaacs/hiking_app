# Step 1.4 — Duration estimator

Branch: `step/1.4-duration-estimator` · Phase: 1 · Status: tests

## Goal
A pure `estimate(route, start_time, pace_factor, rest_min_per_hour)` turns a parsed `RouteSummary` into a `Timing`
(start, finish, moving and total minutes, an ETA for every point) using Tobler's hiking function. After one pace factor
is set, it lands within ±20 % of the manager's real time on 5 past hikes.

## Checkpoint
Verbatim from the roadmap: **on 5 hikes you've done, after setting your pace factor, the estimate is within ±20% of your real time**

How it's measured:
- The manager supplies 5 hikes, each with an AllTrails `.gpx` of the route, the date, the start time, and the **total
  elapsed time** (start to finish, breaks included). They go into `docs/maps/hikes/`, uncommitted.
- QA's harness (`api/tools/timing_checkpoint.py`, the same style as `api/tools/elevation_grid.py`) parses each file
  through the real `summarise_*` pipeline. It runs `estimate` with the default rest (10 min/h) and pace 1.0.
- One pace factor for all 5 is fitted as the geometric mean of real ÷ estimated. Total time is linear in the pace
  factor, so this fit is exact to reason about.
- The bar is |error| ≤ 20 % for every hike at the fitted factor. The harness prints the factor and each hike's error;
  the numbers go into `PROGRESS.md`. The GPX files are not committed.

## Out of scope
- Any endpoint or request field for timing (`start_time`, `pace_factor` in a request). That arrives with
  `ChecklistRequest` (1.10) and `TripContext` (1.6).
- Weather sample-point selection (1.5) and the timezone of the forecast (1.5). `estimate` takes an aware `start_time`
  and does no timezone conversion.
- Calibrating from actual times in the app (2.8). Group pace profiles (4.3).
- Re-tuning the elevation smoothing (ADR 0005 follow-up).

## Decisions and deviations
- 2026-11-02 — Tech lead: the checkpoint fit is one pace factor (geometric mean of ratios) against the total elapsed time
  at the default rest. Hike GPX files stay uncommitted (they're the manager's own data).
- 2026-11-02 — Tech lead accepts ADR 0006 (proposed → accepted for build). Re-checked: flat 5 km 59.5623 min / 69.4894 min
  with rest 10; the spike case's two changed segments (s = ±0.1875). `assumed_flat` on `Timing` **rejected** (beyond the
  roadmap; `route.elevation_source` already carries it). The public `segment_slopes` helper and UTC ETA arithmetic
  **accepted** (no new fields, no tz policy). The 100 m window and 0.5 clamp are reasoned, not fitted; if the checkpoint
  fails, report errors per hike at a few values before amending (no fitting to the 5 hikes).
